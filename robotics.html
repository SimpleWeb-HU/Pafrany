Pilot start

A robot residence is a home designed so that humanoid robots can work in it. At Páfrány Boutique Residences a pilot of humanoid robot services for residents is being prepared. This would make it the first residential development in Europe with such a service. The robots work in the shared spaces as an operated service. Residents do not have to buy or maintain them. The pilot builds on the smart home technology at Páfrány and is planned for residents of the thirty residences on Páfrány út in Buda.
