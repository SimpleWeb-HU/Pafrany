Lilongwe Property Kft.

Lilongwe Property is the Budapest real estate developer of Páfrány Boutique Residences, which it first presented in 2021 as Apáthy Park. Under the 2022 urban planning agreement the developer has committed to a footbridge over the Ördög-árok, road and pavement works, a local history exhibition in the listed house and Natura 2000 protection measures.
