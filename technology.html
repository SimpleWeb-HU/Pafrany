Building

Every residence at Páfrány Boutique Residences is handed over turnkey, in A+ energy class, with one smart home system that brings climate, light, shading, security and energy together. The building is also planned with humanoid robot services in mind. The kitchens come with the full Gaggenau appliance suite in all thirty residences.

Quiet technology

Light scenes, shading, heating and cooling respond to the time of day and to the people at home. Everything can be set by hand, on a wall panel or from a phone. The home also runs well without any settings.

A house with room for robots

The shared spaces are planned so that service robots can move and work in them. [Infrastructure details to be confirmed.] The humanoid robot service itself runs as a pilot.

About the robotics pilot

What is built into every residence

  • Reynaers HiFinity glazing

    Thermally broken aluminium frames with large lift-and-slide doors and triple warm-edge glazing (Ug 0.6 W/m²K), with RC2 security glass on the ground floor. Motorised external blinds with bronze slats provide shading.

  • Room-by-room climate

    Every residence has its own heating and cooling: an air-to-water heat pump, underfloor heating and ceiling cooling. Temperature and shading are set on the wall panel or from a phone, as part of the integrated smart home.

  • Quiet between homes

    Party walls are 25 cm precast reinforced concrete with a plasterboard lining. Every room has a floating screed on two layers of impact sound insulation, the stairs rest on vibration-damping pads and entrance doors are rated at least 35 dB.
