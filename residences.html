Páfrány Boutique Residences is a boutique luxury development of thirty new-build residences in Budapest’s 2nd district, on Páfrány út near Pasarét. Every home has a private garden or terrace and a full Gaggenau kitchen, and is handed over turnkey in A+ energy class. Every residence also comes with a private storage room and two spaces in the underground garage. Prices are not published. Register your interest to receive floor plans and availability.
