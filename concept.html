The home of the future, in a private park in Buda

Páfrány Boutique Residences is six villas and thirty residences in a landscaped park on Páfrány út, beside the Apáthy rock. Every residence is built to the highest standard, with technologies offered in a home here for the first time, and residents have a wellness area of their own. The name comes from Páfrány út, the street it stands on (páfrány is Hungarian for fern).

From Apáthy Park to Páfrány

Páfrány Boutique Residences is the realised form of the development that Lilongwe Property first presented in 2021 as Apáthy Park, at the corner of Páfrány út and Szalonka út. Under the 2022 urban planning agreement the developer has committed to a footbridge over the Ördög-árok, road and pavement works, a local history exhibition in the listed house and Natura 2000 protection measures for lighting and fencing.

About the project

Is Páfrány a branded residence?

No. Páfrány Boutique Residences is a private residence without a hotel brand or operator. The wellness area and the porter service are for residents only.

What is included for residents?

A wellness area, a 24-hour porter service, an underground garage and an integrated smart home. Every residence has a full Gaggenau kitchen as standard, and a pilot of humanoid robot services is being prepared. [Wellness details to be confirmed.]

How is it related to Apáthy Park?

Apáthy Park was the name under which the developer, Lilongwe Property, first presented the project in 2021. Páfrány Boutique Residences is the same development on the same site, now being realised.

Is it the same as the Páfrány 9 Residence project?

No. Páfrány Boutique Residences at Páfrány út 17/b is a separate project from Páfrány 9 Residence at Páfrány utca 9, with a different developer, plot and building.

Who is the developer?

Lilongwe Property Kft., a Budapest real estate developer. Company details are on the contact page.

What will the listed house be used for?

The house from 1820 is preserved at the centre of the park. The developer will announce its future use once it has been decided.

Which nature protection commitments apply?

The 2022 urban planning agreement includes Natura 2000 protection measures for lighting and fencing. The site lies outside the protection zone of the Apáthy rock nature reserve. [Wording to be confirmed.]
