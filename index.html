A residence far from the towers

Páfrány Boutique Residences is being built in Budapest’s District II, on Páfrány út, beside the protected Apáthy rock. Six small villas surround a listed house from 1820, the first house of the Buda villa quarter. Every home has a private garden or terrace.

The concept

Six villas, one house from 1820

The villas step down the slope so that every home opens onto green: garden residences at ground level, terrace residences above, and penthouses with roof terraces facing the Apáthy rock. The 1820 inn at the centre of the site is being preserved.
