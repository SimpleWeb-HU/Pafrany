Penthouses, one per villa

Each of the six villas at Páfrány Boutique Residences has one penthouse on its top floor. The 123 m² of living space comes with a private 235 m² roof terrace on Páfrány út, in Budapest’s District II, at the foot of the protected Apáthy rock. Of the penthouses for sale, the penthouse in villa A1 is currently available. Garden and terrace apartments are on the residences page.

A garden above the treetops

The roof terrace is almost twice the size of the apartment: 235 m² with room for outdoor dining, a lounge area and planting. It looks out over the wooded hillside and the Apáthy rock, a protected nature area since 1977. [Terrace layout and fittings to be confirmed by the developer.]

Included in every penthouse

The penthouses share the specification of all residences and are handed over turnkey. Each kitchen has the full Gaggenau appliance suite, and one smart home system runs climate, lighting, shading and security.

Heating and cooling come from an air-to-water heat pump with underfloor heating and ceiling cooling. The large glazed walls use Reynaers HiFinity frames with triple glazing, shaded by motorised bronze external blinds.
